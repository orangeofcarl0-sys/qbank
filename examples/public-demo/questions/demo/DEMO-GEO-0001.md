## 题目

The diagram shows a square whose side length is one unit. What is its area?

![Self-authored unit-square diagram](assets/images/unit-square.svg)

## 选项

- A. 1 square unit
- B. 2 square units
- C. 4 square units
- D. It cannot be determined

## 答案

A

## 解析

The area of a square is side multiplied by side, so $1\times1=1$ square unit.

## 评分要点

Select A.
